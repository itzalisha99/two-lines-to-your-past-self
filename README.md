Entry for the make.fun bounty [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

Two lines sent back to a past self. Line 1 warns about one specific
moment — a sentence said out loud on a platform at 6:47. Line 2 says why
that moment mattered and what changed because of it: that was the last
time her number lit up the phone.

> Don't tell her 'I'll call you back' on the platform at 6:47.
> That was the last time her number lit up your phone.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (113 characters, 2 lines).
